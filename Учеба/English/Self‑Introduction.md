Let's be brutally honest, Skolkovo is a big step in my life. Looking back, when I was a fourteen-old child who likes videos about entrepreneurs I couldn't even image to be in a such place. 

In my childhood environment wasn't so motivating to develop skills and conciseness. My mother was the only one who believed in me. Because of her I study here, have a high-paid job in Fintech industry and live the best life. She taught me to like risk and to be responsible for my actions. That's why in my spare time i like climbing and trekking across my country. She formed my mind in a way of becoming better permanently.

I think being at Skolkovo is a defining moment in my life. My main aim is to disrupt the industry and help people to live much more comfortable life without worries that you cannot achieve something. It only takes a single decision to change everything.
