# Essay on topic «What's the cost of career»
What does it mean to build a career in the modern world? According to Hoffman, a career is not simply a job. It is a continuous process of personal growth and adaptation to the changing market. Throughout this process, a person develops significant skills, adapt their mindset, and seeks new opportunities for self-improvement. As Hoffman suggests, this flow reflects a deep human desire to «leave this world better than you found it». This essay will explore ideas from the book and give my personal answer on the question in the title.

Hoffman believes that every person should treat their career as a startup. According to his view, the biggest cost of it isn't money, time, relationships and etc. It's refusal to adapt mindset to the new environment. A person who stops adaptation always becomes the last one in the leaderboard of life. 

But how people understand the requirements of success and how don't become a loser? First of all, you should constantly compare own aspirations and market realities. The market is really changeable thus to keep your finger on the pulse is more significant than ever.

Nevertheless, people are all different. Our talents, skills, possibilities are different. Some people cannot just feel and keep this pace of life. However, they may be good executioners and workers. Maybe there is a kind of people who like just relax, chill and feel freshness of the air. And, of course, they have the right to live and spend their time on simple things. 

To sum up, Hoffman's view has a lot of advantages, inspirations and belief in continuous development. But there are enormous people in the world and not all of them are able to build a startup. Some of them are born to have small aims and dreams. While others are here to disrupt the world and change the life.
